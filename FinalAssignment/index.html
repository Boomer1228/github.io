This is the very unofficial fan page of developer FromSoftware's hit video game genre, SoulsBourne.

This video game genre was essentially created and refined over the years by FromSoftware. It is an action game which is made to be incredibly difficult. The purpose of the games is to provide a meaningful challenge which the player can overcome in multiple ways depending on the type of weapon or class that they choose for themselves as the player.


Some of the main gameplay elements include:

  • Character Selection and Leveling System
  • Fantastic Enemy Designs
  • A Beautifully Interconnected World
  • Deep and Intriguing World-Building Lore
  • A Dodge and Parry System
